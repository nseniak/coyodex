The drawer keeps the drawing clear; the card sits over it and can be dragged. Stored only in this browser.

Getting around the map

  • Select a view at the top of the graph pane
  • Pinch to zoom, scroll or drag to move
  • Click a box or arrow to see its details
  • ⌘-click (Cmd / Ctrl) another box or arrow — adds it to the selection
  • Double-click or ⌥-click (Option / Alt) a container to drill into it
  • Shift-click a box or arrow to zoom in and center it
  • Press / (or ⌘K) to search every element, file and term in the map

# The closing sequence runs as `coyomap ship`, not thirteen hand-run commands

Change: a new `coyomap ship` verb runs the closing list's steps 2–12 (prepare without
--note-file, ending on the grounding report; finish with it, through finalize), stopping at the
first failing step and naming every step that did not run; the method's ordering block now leads
with the verb and keeps the list as reference · tools/coyomap/ship.py, tools/coyomap/cli.py,
method.md. Motive: on real builds the hand-run tail was redone (finalize run twice, the grounding
record written twice, a heredoc as the last write) — the 2026-08-27 method assessment, item C4.

Escalation: none — ship adds no check; every gate it runs still gates itself.

## Checks

1. expect: the next build's transcript closes with two `coyomap ship` invocations (prepare, then
   finish) and zero hand-run occurrences of the steps ship owns (`anchor-drift`, `fix
   apply-drift`, the closing assembles, `grounding write`, `provenance stamp`, the final
   `validate`/`audit`/`render`/`finalize`) outside a ship refusal the build explains.
   regression sign: the tail is hand-run with ship available, or ship refused and the build worked
   around it silently instead of saying why.
2. expect: the sequence's known failure shapes do not recur — exactly one `finalize` run, exactly
   one `grounding write` after the reconcile, no hand-written `built` minute, and the grounding
   note written AFTER the prepare phase's report.
   regression sign: a duplicated or re-ordered closing step, which is exactly what the verb exists
   to make impossible.
3. expect: when a step fails mid-ship, the build fixes that step's own report and re-runs ship,
   and the transcript shows the "NOT RUN" list from the failed run.
   regression sign: a failed ship followed by hand-running the remaining steps, which reintroduces
   the skipped-step-reads-as-clean risk.
